* 320 g Arborio or Carnaroli rice
* 1 medium onion, finely diced
* 2 garlic cloves, minced
* 1–2 tbsp olive oil or butter
* 200 g mushrooms, sliced
* 150 ml dry white wine
* 900 ml vegetable stock
* 80 g Parmesan, finely grated
* 2 tbsp butter
* thyme
* nutmeg
* salt & pepper

1. Set the pressure cooker to **Sauté**. Add the oil or butter and cook the onion for **3–4 minutes**, until softened.
2. Add the garlic and mushrooms, if using. Cook for another **2 minutes**.
3. Add the rice and stir for **1 minute** to lightly toast it.
4. Add the white wine, if using, and let it bubble for **30–60 seconds**.
5. Pour in **900 ml–1 litre vegetable stock**. Scrape the bottom thoroughly to make sure nothing is stuck.
6. Close and seal the pressure cooker. Cook on **High Pressure for 6 minutes**.
7. Perform a **quick pressure release**.
8. Open the cooker and stir the risotto vigorously for **30–60 seconds**.
9. Stir in the Parmesan and butter. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.
