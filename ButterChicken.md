Marinade

* chicken 400
* lemon juice 1 tbl
* chili powder
* salt

* yoghurt 150
* tumeric 1/2 ts
* kasuri methi 1 ts
* garam masala 1 ts
* oil 1 tbl
* ginger garlic paste 1 tbl

Gravy

* butter 30
* clove 3
* cardamon a bit
* cinnamon 1 ts
* ginger garlic paste 1 tbl

* tomato puree 400
* cashew 20 pieces
* sugar 1 ts

* water 200

* garam 1 ts
* kasuri methi 1 ts
* coriander a bit
* cream 100


1. First marination for 20 minutes, then second overnight
2. For gravy, start by saute the first group
3. On second pan heat the marinade so all moisture is gone
4. In the meantime puree tomatos with cashews and sugar
5. Put the puree and heat it to make it thick
6. Add the water and keep it for 3 minutes, then add chicken, keep it another 5 minutes
7. Add the spices and finish off with the cream
